FAQ

Quick answers.

Is TaskFlow free?

Yes. It is a free student project with no sign-up or subscription.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser using local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.

Will I lose tasks if I close the tab?

No. Tasks stay saved until you delete them or clear your browser data.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.
